# Research Boundary

This repository is for simulation-only, preregistered computational research.

It does not provide hardware control, medical advice, financial execution, physical validation, consciousness claims, autonomous authority, or deployment approval.

Every result must:

1. Reference an immutable preregistration hash.
2. Identify its evidence level.
3. Preserve finite inputs and outputs.
4. Carry the statement: “simulation result; not empirical or physical validation.”
5. Remain reproducible before any broader claim is considered.
